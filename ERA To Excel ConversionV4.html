Denial Claim Workbench

CPT ↔ DX linkage & denial resolution · Coastal Heart Medical Group

Upload EOBs / remits / claim files — drag & drop here or . Accepts raw 835 ERA files (.835/.edi/.era/.txt from Availity, Waystar, TriZetto, Office Ally or any clearinghouse), PDF EOBs from any PM system (text or scanned), ZIP batches, Excel/CSV exports, Word (.docx) and images.
Best accuracy: upload the raw 835 file — every PM system (AdvancedMD, Athena, Kareo/Tebra, eCW, NextGen, Epic, CareCloud, DrChrono, Office Ally, WebPT …) receives the same 835, so it reads exactly. Printed PDF EOBs are read by automatic column detection; the upload log shows the format detected and whether totals match the document.
Files are read only inside this browser — nothing is uploaded to a server. PDF, Excel, Word and image reading uses free libraries downloaded from a CDN, so an internet connection is needed for those. Scanned PDFs and images are read with OCR (slower). Auto-extracted values should be verified against the EOB.
